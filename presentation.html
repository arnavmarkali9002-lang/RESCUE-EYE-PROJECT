2. The Core Problem: Why Floods Turn Fatal

Problem Statement
⚠️ 3 Fatal Bottlenecks in Current Disaster Response
1.
Late Warnings (Manual Delays): Traditional alerts rely on manual phone calls and paperwork, arriving 2 to 4 hours after riverbanks have already overflowed.
2.
Blind Rescue Operations: First responders (NDRF / SDRF) have zero live aerial visibility of flooded streets. They don't know who is trapped or where survivors are until phone calls arrive.
3.
Resource Sizing Guesswork: Commanders guess how many boats or rescuers to send — causing either tragic shortages or wasted emergency assets.
🗣️ What to Say (20-Second Script)
"In India, floods cause massive casualties because existing warnings arrive hours too late. Rescue teams are dispatched blindly without knowing where victims are or how many people need help. A 4-hour advance warning can reduce deaths by 78%, which is the exact problem Rescue Eye solves."
📊 Ground Reality & Real-World Impact
🌊
40+ Million Hectares are severely flood-prone across India (e.g. Godavari Basin at Kopargaon, Mithi River at Mumbai).
💸
₹50,000+ Crores lost every year in destroyed roads, bridges, power grids, and submerged farmland.
⚡
The Life-Saving Lead Time: Research proves that a 4-hour advance warning reduces casualty rates by up to 78%!
💡
Zero Hardware Cost: Rescue Eye runs 100% on open web APIs and client AI — no expensive IoT sensors required.

🚤 Phase 3: DISPATCH (Rescue Action)
Counts victims, auto-selects the right team (QRU, Motorboat, Amphibious), and displays a live approach countdown clock.

5. Step 2: Explainable AI (Why is Risk High?)

Transparency & Trust
💡 Eliminating the "Black Box" Mystery
❓
The Problem with Standard AI: Standard neural networks output a number without explaining why. Disaster officers cannot order costly evacuations without evidence.
✅
Explainable AI (XAI) Solution: Rescue Eye breaks down every prediction into physical, auditable drivers (Rainfall, River, Elevation, History).
⚖️
Legal & Operational Auditability: Municipal collectors and NDRF commanders have full proof to justify early evacuation orders.
🗣️ What to Say (20-Second Script)
"Disaster commanders often mistrust AI because standard models don't explain why a risk is high. Rescue Eye uses Explainable AI to show the exact evidence: for example, plus 28% from 68 mm heavy rainfall and plus 24% from Godavari river reaching 94% capacity. This gives officials complete confidence to order evacuations."
📈 Live Factor Attribution Waterfall
🔺 Positive Risk Drivers (Escalating Hazard):
+68 mm Heavy Rainfall: +28% hazard impact (high surface runoff volume).
River at 94% Capacity: +24% hazard impact (overflow imminence).
🔻 Negative Mitigating Factors (Reducing Risk):
High Terrain Slope: -8% natural drainage gravity runoff.
Active Storm Sluices: -6% municipal drainage relief.
